## How to Create an Airbnb Welcome Book (Step-by-Step)

No matter which tool you choose, here's what every great [Airbnb welcome book](/airbnb-house-manual/) should include:

### Step 1: Gather Your Essential Information

Start with what guests ask most:
- **Wi-Fi network and password**
- **Check-in/check-out procedures** (with photos or video)
- **Door codes or key instructions**
- **House rules** (quiet hours, parking, pets, smoking)
- **Emergency contacts** (your number, local hospital, police)

### Step 2: Add Property-Specific Details

- Appliance instructions (coffee maker, smart TV, HVAC, washer/dryer)
- Hot tub or pool rules and instructions
- Trash and recycling schedule
- Thermostat and lighting controls

### Step 3: Include Local Recommendations

This is where your welcome book goes from functional to memorable:
- Top 5 restaurants within walking distance
- Nearest grocery store and pharmacy
- Best coffee shops
- Must-see attractions and hidden gems
- Transportation options (rideshare, public transit, parking)

### Step 4: Set Up Your Delivery Method

- **Digital guidebook (recommended):** Share a link in your booking confirmation, pre-arrival message, and a QR code inside the property
- **Printed binder:** Place in a visible spot (coffee table, kitchen counter)
- **PDF:** Email to guests 2–3 days before arrival

### Step 5: Keep It Updated

The biggest advantage of digital guidebooks over printed ones: you can update information instantly. Set a reminder to review your guide quarterly — check that restaurants are still open, hours are accurate, and seasonal recommendations are current.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I include in an Airbnb welcome book?

At minimum: Wi-Fi credentials, check-in/check-out instructions, house rules, emergency contacts, and appliance guides. The best welcome books also include local restaurant recommendations, transportation options, and a personal welcome message from the host.

### Is a digital or printed Airbnb welcome book better?

Digital guidebooks are better for most hosts in 2026. They're easier to update, accessible on guests' phones, and can include interactive features like maps and AI chat. Printed binders still work well as a supplement, especially for properties where guests may have limited internet access.

### Can I create an Airbnb welcome book for free?

Yes. BnBuddy offers a completely free digital guidebook with an AI assistant. Hostfully provides a free tier for 1 guidebook. Canva has free templates for printed/PDF welcome books. You don't need to spend anything to create a professional welcome book.

### What's the best free alternative to TouchStay?

BnBuddy is the strongest free alternative to Touch Stay. It offers a digital guidebook and AI guest assistant at no cost. Hostfully's free tier (1 guidebook) is another option, though it includes sponsored recommendations.

### What happened to GuestView Guide?

GuestView Guide was discontinued on October 31, 2024. Its parent company, Sharp NEC Display Solutions, shut down the service. Former users should transition to a software-based alternative like BnBuddy (free), Touch Stay, or Hostfully.

### How long should an Airbnb welcome book be?

Keep it comprehensive but scannable. Most effective welcome books are 10–20 pages (printed) or 8–15 sections (digital). Prioritize the information guests need most — Wi-Fi, check-in, rules — at the top, with local recommendations and extras further down.

### Do welcome books actually improve Airbnb reviews?

Yes. A well-organized welcome book reduces friction, answers questions proactively, and shows guests you're a thoughtful host. Hosts who provide clear, accessible property information consistently report fewer negative reviews related to miscommunication or unmet expectations.

### What's the difference between a welcome book and a house manual?

They're often used interchangeably. A "house manual" tends to focus on property operations (how things work), while a "welcome book" is broader — including local recommendations, personal touches, and hospitality elements. The best guides combine both. See our [Airbnb house manual guide](/airbnb-house-manual/) for more.
